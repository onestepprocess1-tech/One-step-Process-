FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you get started.

Does One Step Process provide government registrations?

We provide consultancy and application assistance for selected services. Final approval, issuance and processing timelines are determined by the relevant government department or authorised body.

What documents will I need?

Documents depend on the service, applicant and business structure. Contact us first to discuss the applicable checklist.

Can you guarantee a DPIIT certificate or government grant?

No. Recognition, funding, subsidies and grants depend on official eligibility rules, scrutiny, availability and the relevant authority's decision. We do not guarantee approval or funding.

Are government fees included in consultancy charges?

Not automatically. The service scope, professional charges and any applicable official or third-party fees should be confirmed before work begins.

How do I start?

Submit the enquiry form or contact us on WhatsApp with your name, city and required service. Do not send passwords, OTPs or sensitive identity documents in an initial enquiry.
